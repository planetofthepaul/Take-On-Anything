How to remove a background

Drop a photo into the box above or click to choose one from your device. The tool finds the main subject, erases everything behind it, and gives you a transparent PNG you can download and place on any background. The first run loads the processing model into your browser, which takes a few seconds; after that, each image is quick.

Your images stay private

Unlike most background removers, this one does all the work inside your own browser. Your photo is never uploaded to a server and never leaves your device, which makes it a safe choice for product shots, ID photos, personal pictures, or anything you'd rather not send to a third party.

What you can use it for

  • Product photos — drop your item onto a clean white or branded background for a shop listing.
  • Profile pictures — cut yourself out for an avatar, badge, or team page.
  • Stickers and memes — make a transparent cut-out to drop into other images.
  • Presentations and design — place a subject onto slides, posters, or thumbnails without a boxy background.

Tips for the cleanest cut-out

  • Use a photo where the subject stands out clearly from what's behind it.
  • Good, even lighting helps the edges come out sharp.
  • Very fine detail like flyaway hair or fur can be tricky — simpler outlines cut out best.
  • Preview the result against white and dark backgrounds (the buttons above) to check the edges before downloading.

Fine-tune the result by hand

Automatic removal gets most images right, but tricky edges sometimes need a touch-up. After the background is removed, use the built-in brush to clean it up: Erase paints away anything the tool left behind, and Restore brings back any part of the subject that was removed by mistake. Adjust the brush size with the slider, and use Undo to step back. All editing happens live on the image before you download.

Is it really free?

Yes. There's no signup, no watermark, and no limit on how many images you can process. Because everything runs in your browser, there are no per-image costs and nothing to pay.
